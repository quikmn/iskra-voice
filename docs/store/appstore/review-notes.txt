Iskra has no sign-up: an account is a key created on the device. Servers are run by their users (there is no central Iskra service), so the app needs an invite link to a server. We run a review server for you.

HOW TO GET IN
1. Open the app, tick "I agree to the Terms of Use" and tap "I'm new to Iskra". Pick any name.
2. Tap "I have an invite link" and paste: {{INVITE}}
   (Or open the link on the device; it opens the app.)
3. Tap Join. You're in the server's #general channel. Tap the menu button at the top left for the channels (text and voice, e.g. "Hangout") and your direct messages.
No username or password is needed. Screen sharing and starting your own server are only in the desktop app. Notifications arrive while the app is open (no push).

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (GUIDELINE 1.2)
- Terms: everyone accepts the Terms of Use (https://iskra.foo/terms, including community guidelines with zero tolerance for objectionable content and abuse) before using the app.
- Report: every message and every person has "Report" in its menu (the message's "..." menu, or tap a person). Reports go to that server's moderators, who see a report queue and can delete the content, time the person out, kick or ban them; the reported person never sees who reported them.
- Block: "Block" in a person's menu stops their DMs and calls on every shared server; My settings > Blocked people manages the list.
- Moderation tools: roles and permissions, message deletion (one message or up to 100 at once), warnings, timeouts, kick and ban (optionally deleting the person's messages from the last day or week), private moderator notes and each member's moderation history, invites that need a moderator's approval, pausing all invites, a slowmode for newcomers, slow mode, AutoMod (blocked words, links, spam, join floods), and an activity log.
- Contact: the Iskra team acts on safety reports sent to spark@iskra.foo (https://iskra.foo/support#safety), including servers whose moderators don't act. On servers we run (like this review server), we remove offending content and people ourselves.

OTHER NOTES
- Voice keeps working with the screen locked (background mode "audio"); a call you're in shows through CallKit.
- Encryption: standard algorithms only (libsodium, TLS, DTLS-SRTP), exempt; ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is false.
- No tracking, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
