**Iskra: alpha 10.**

### What's new
- **Report messages and people.** Right-click a message or a person → Report. Server moderators handle reports from a new **Reports** queue (with a counter), and the person you report never learns it was you. In encrypted DMs you choose exactly which messages to share.
- **Terms of use and community guidelines.** You'll be asked to agree once. Zero tolerance for abuse, and every server's moderators have the tools to act.
- **Delete your account** from Settings → Account: it removes you from every server, and can delete your messages too.
- **New pages:** [terms](https://iskra.foo/terms), [privacy](https://iskra.foo/privacy), [support](https://iskra.foo/support) and [how to delete your account](https://iskra.foo/delete-account).
- **Support Iskra** with Ko-fi or PayPal from Settings → About, if you'd like to.
- The Mac app is signed and notarized by Apple: open it like any other app.

### Fixes
- When someone declines your call, it now says "Declined" instead of "Cancelled".
- iskra.foo no longer loads fonts from Google.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.10.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** the `iskra-server` packages. Run `iskra-server my-server` and open the owner link it prints.

### Good to know
- Apps from alpha 3 and later update themselves. Servers don't: download the new `iskra-server` and restart it. Reports and account deletion need the new server.
- Mac apps from before alpha 9 can't update themselves to the signed one: download the new `.dmg` once.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and check where a file was built with `gh attestation verify <file> -R quikmn/iskra-voice`.
