### What's new
- Themes: all of them reworked so menus sit above the panel they open from, hover and selected rows show, text steps down clearly, and red only ever means danger. New: Iskra Light and Midnight (true black for OLED screens). Theme cards show a small picture of the app in that look.
- Fonts are now their own setting (My settings → Appearance → Font): Inter, System, Rounded, Serif or Monospace, plus a code font. Inter, Nunito and JetBrains Mono ship with the app, so the text looks the same on every device. Built-in themes no longer change your font; a theme file that names one does while "Use the theme's font when it names one" is on.
- A tidier look across settings and dialogs: real switches for things that take effect at once, an unsaved-changes bar for forms, My settings in groups with icons, one place for notices, menus with icons and submenus, the member sheet's moderation in tabs, and bigger touch targets on phones.
- A calmer app frame: the server menu is shorter (the server, you here, leave; everything else under More settings, in groups) with your notifications on one page; the member list has a header with a count and a search; switching or adding accounts lives in your status menu, and the rail shows a stacked account tile only when you have more than one; the recovery key reminder is a slim line, then a dot on your avatar; Invite friends is a big card while you are alone; a server that can't be reached or waits for approval shows one centred card.

### Fixes
- White text and icons on red (hang up, unread badges, recording) and on green (accept, join) now read clearly in every theme, and the keyboard focus ring shows in every theme.
- Clearer words across the app, in English and Swedish: one name for each thing (direct messages and group chats, permissions, built-in roles, the audit log, "My settings →" paths), error messages that say what to do next, and the same quotes, dashes and time formats everywhere.

### Good to know
- Fonts are now their own setting: if a theme used to give you a different font (Graphite, Solarized, Synthwave, Cyber-Lime), you now see Inter; pick another in My settings → Appearance → Font.
