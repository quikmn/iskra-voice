### What's new
- Events is a calendar now: a month view (the default on a computer) and an agenda (the default on a phone), with repeating events on every day they fall on, birthdays, and each time shown in your own time zone. Click an empty day to plan something on it.
- Add to my calendar: get a private link for a server and subscribe to it in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar, and that server's events show up there and stay up to date. Off until you make a link; make a new one or turn it off at any time. Google can take up to a day to show a change, Outlook a few hours.
- In the Android app, "Put this server's events in my phone's calendar" writes events straight into a calendar on your phone, where they show up within seconds and sync to your Google or Outlook account. Changed and cancelled events follow; turning it off removes them.

### Good to know
- Calendar links need a server that calendar services can reach over https from the internet; on other servers the app says so instead of giving a link that can't work. Owners can turn calendar links off in Server settings, Overview.
