### What's new
- The iPhone app follows your phone's language in all nine languages, and Android's "In voice" notification is translated too.

### Fixes
- Phones: in a direct or group call on a small screen, or with the keyboard open, the message box stays on screen. Answering a call with the channel list open now shows the call.
- Typing a long message no longer moves the chat off its newest message, and the message box in direct messages and groups grows with its text.
- Small phones: press-and-hold on a message shows all its actions (the list scrolls).
- Phones: larger tap areas for buttons, tabs and channel rows, no tap flash, and the inbox no longer cuts titles mid-word.
- Phone held sideways: settings and the picture viewer keep clear of the notch, and tapping a field no longer zooms in on iPhone.

### Good to know
- iPhone builds now use Xcode 27 (the iOS 27 SDK), as the App Store will require from April 2027.
