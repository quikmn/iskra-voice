### What's new
- **Rent your own server on DigitalOcean from the desktop app.** Create a server → Rent one on DigitalOcean → Connect to DigitalOcean, pick a region, and Iskra creates, secures and sets up the server in your own account, makes you its owner and gives you a document explaining your setup (with a ready prompt for an AI helper). You pay DigitalOcean directly, about $6 a month; Iskra never sees your server, key or card.
- **See exactly which version you run.** The window title shows the version and build ("Iskra 2.0.0-alpha.15 · 1a2b3c4"), and My settings → About & legal has it with a copy button for bug reports.
- **The desktop app wears the same icon as the phone apps**, in the title bar, taskbar and tray.

### Fixes
- Clicking into a channel no longer draws a blue frame around the messages; the focus frame shows only when you move around with Tab.

### Good to know
- Renting on DigitalOcean goes through Iskra's DigitalOcean referral link: new accounts get DigitalOcean's signup credit, and Iskra gets a little DigitalOcean credit that helps pay for its own server. The app says so before you sign up.
