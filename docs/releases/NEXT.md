### What's new
- A catch-up when quiet hours end: a short "While it was quiet" card lists the mentions, messages, alert words, events and reminders you missed across all your servers, each a click away, and phones get one summary notification instead of a pile. Turn it off in the quiet hours settings.
- A public page for your server: open its address in a browser and people see your group's name, picture, a short description and a Join button, served by your own server. It's off until you turn it on in Server settings → Overview, and you choose exactly what it shows.
- Encrypted voice that keeps going: in an encrypted voice channel, voice, video and screen shares are encrypted on your devices, a badge shows when they are, and the key changes whenever someone loses access without dropping the call. Clips from those channels are encrypted too.

### Fixes
- Calls say "Encrypted call", with the full explanation when you point at it. An encrypted call now refuses any media that arrives unencrypted.
