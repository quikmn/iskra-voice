### What's new
- Join sounds: pick a short sound from a server's soundboard (My settings → Voice & audio) and the others in a voice channel hear it when you enter. You can turn other people's join sounds off for yourself; the owner can turn them off for a server.
- Read receipts for direct messages and group chats, off by default (My settings → Privacy & data). They are mutual: you see "Seen" only if you send receipts too.
- Private names: rename a person for yourself only (right-click them → Private name). It shows everywhere in your app and on your other devices; nobody else sees it.
- Mute several servers at once: a server's Notifications menu → Mute several servers.
- Hide buttons you don't use (My settings → Appearance): GIFs, emoji, the voice message mic, threads, pins, search, call buttons and the voice bar's soundboard. Each stays reachable from a menu.
- Photos: a new kind of channel for shared photo and video albums. A fast grid by month, a filter by person, newest or oldest first, albums with covers, and a viewer with zoom, swipe, captions, reactions and comments. Add many at once (drop them on the channel, or pick them on a phone); each file shows its progress.
- Photos keep their full quality. Location, camera and phone details and other hidden data are removed from every photo and video before it is stored; the date taken stays unless you turn that off when adding.
- "Download album" saves an album as a zip. Select many photos to move or delete them; moderators can delete any photo. Admins can set a storage limit per photos channel ("1.2 GB of 5 GB used").
- A photos channel can be end-to-end encrypted, like other private channels of up to 50 people: photos, thumbnails, captions and album names are encrypted on your device.
- Share photos from your phone's gallery straight into a photos channel (Share → Iskra).

### Good to know
- Photos channels take JPEG, PNG and WebP pictures and MP4 or MOV videos. GIF, WebM, HEIC and AVIF aren't taken yet (an iPhone set to "Most Compatible" takes JPEG). Photos channels need the server updated.
- In an encrypted photos channel, people who join later can't see photos added before they could read the channel, as with messages.
- With read receipts on, a server that carries the conversation can tell that you read it and when, not which message. Join sounds need the server updated; an older server shows no join sound settings.
