**Iskra: alpha 30.**

### What's new
- Backup buddies: your server's daily backups can also go to a friend's Iskra server, encrypted on your server first, so your friend keeps them but can't read them. If your computer is ever lost, restore the whole server from there with your recovery code, right from the desktop app. You choose how much space you give a friend in return.
- Play together in voice: start Draw & guess, a quiz or Four in a row right in the call, and everyone in the channel can join with one tap. Server owners can add their own activities, which run walled off from the internet in the desktop app.
- A public page for your server: open its address in a browser and people see your group's name, picture, a short description and a Join button, served by your own server. It's off until you turn it on in Server settings → Overview, and you choose exactly what it shows.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.30.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** create one in the desktop app, rent one from the app, or run the `iskra-server` package: `iskra-server my-server`, then open the owner link it prints. The [hosting guide](https://github.com/quikmn/iskra-voice/blob/main/docs/hosting.md) has every step.
