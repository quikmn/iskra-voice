**Iskra: alpha 37.**

### What's new
- Share to Iskra on iPhone and iPad too: pick Iskra in the share sheet, choose a channel, DM or group, and it's sent the normal way, end-to-end encrypted where the conversation is.

### Fixes
- The account picture at the bottom of the server list sits evenly in its circle.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.37.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** create one in the desktop app, rent one from the app, or run the `iskra-server` package: `iskra-server my-server`, then open the owner link it prints. The [hosting guide](https://iskra.foo/hosting) has every step.
