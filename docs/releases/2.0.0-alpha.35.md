**Iskra: alpha 35.**

### What's new
- Choose who can message you: anyone, anyone as a message request, only people you've talked to (plus server staff), or nobody new. Message requests wait in their own list without notifications, with links and files held back until you accept, and the sender never learns whether you ignored them.

- Join questions: new members answer a few friendly questions right after joining. Each answer can give them roles and point them to the right channels, and they can change their answers any time. Admins can also pick roles everyone gets on join, and those can never carry permissions.

- Favorite channels: star channels from any server and find them under Favorites at the top of Home. The list follows your account to your other devices, encrypted.

- Tables in messages: GitHub-style tables (`| a | b |`) show as real tables and scroll sideways inside the message on a phone.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.35.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** create one in the desktop app, rent one from the app, or run the `iskra-server` package: `iskra-server my-server`, then open the owner link it prints. The [hosting guide](https://iskra.foo/hosting) has every step.

### Good to know
- Server pick groups ("Pick your roles") are now join questions; existing groups carry over as they were.
