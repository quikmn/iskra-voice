**Iskra: alpha 9.**

### What's new
- **Friend requests, like on Discord.** Right-click anyone (or press and hold on your phone) → Send friend request; they accept or ignore. Pending requests show up under Friends, with a badge. Requests are end-to-end encrypted and signed, so servers never see your friends list.
- **A people menu everywhere.** Right-click a person in the member list, chat, DMs, voice channels or calls: profile, message, call, friend, volume and mute, block, copy ID, and moderation for moderators.
- **Add friend** right in the DM header.
- **Sign out of a device** in Settings → Devices. Iskra warns you first if it's your last device and you haven't saved your recovery key, and can leave your servers too.
- **New devices ask up front:** new to Iskra, or already using it on another device? Linking instead of making a second account by accident.

### Fixes
- When someone stops sharing their screen, viewers no longer get stuck on a black tile, and text channels always take you back to chat.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.9.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** the `iskra-server` packages. Run `iskra-server my-server` and open the owner link it prints.

### Good to know
- Apps from alpha 3 and later update themselves. Servers don't: download the new `iskra-server` and restart it.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
