**Iskra: alpha 7.**

### What's new
- **Soundboard sounds of any length.** No more 5-second cap, and a **Stop sounds** button in the soundboard while something plays.
- **Settings → Limits** (server owner): set the largest upload, daily upload allowances, and soundboard, emoji and sticker limits right in the app. Changes apply instantly and are saved in `server.json`.
- **Much higher limits out of the box:** 512 MB uploads, 10 GB a day per member, soundboard sounds of any length and size, 1 MB emoji and 4 MB stickers.
- Calls no longer show "Part of the call couldn't be decrypted" for the moment someone leaves.
- Servers shut down right away instead of waiting on connected apps.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.7.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** the `iskra-server` packages. Run `iskra-server my-server` and open the owner link it prints.

### Good to know
- Apps from alpha 3 and later update themselves.
- Servers that already have a `server.json` keep their upload limit; raise it in Settings → Limits.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
