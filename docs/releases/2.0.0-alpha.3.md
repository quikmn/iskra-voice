**Iskra: alpha 3.** Voice and text chat for your friend group, on a server you own. The biggest update yet.

### What's new
- **Open to the internet with one switch:** the app sets up your router, gives your server a free address and a real HTTPS certificate. Friends join from anywhere, in the app or the browser.
- **Mac and Linux apps**, and **automatic updates**: new versions download in the background, one click to restart.
- **Voice and video calls in DMs**, end-to-end encrypted.
- **Pins, search, threads, polls, custom emoji and a soundboard.**
- **Bots and webhooks:** works with tools that post to Discord-style webhooks, plus GitHub, and a small kit for writing your own bots.
- **Link a new device** with a QR code, see and remove your devices. DMs now have forward secrecy.
- **Profiles:** picture, bio, pronouns, accent colour and your own profile page. **11 themes.**
- **Phones:** install the web app to your home screen and get push notifications for mentions, replies, DMs and calls.
- A fresh look across desktop and phone, reorganised settings, Ctrl+K quick switcher, press-and-hold actions on phones.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.3.exe` (or the portable `.exe`). **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. Click **Create a server** and you're the owner.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo), on PC and phones.
- **Your own server:** the `iskra-server` packages for Windows and Linux. Run `iskra-server my-server` and open the owner link it prints.

### Good to know
- This is an alpha: expect rough edges, and tell us what you find.
- The Windows app and server are code-signed (Open Source Developer Viktor Lundgren). The Mac app isn't notarized yet: right-click → Open the first time.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
