**Iskra: alpha 46.**

### What's new
- Android app: voice keeps going in the background with an "In voice" notification (with Leave), sound comes from the loudspeaker with a switch to the earpiece, and you can share photos and files from other apps to Iskra.
- Phones: hanging up shows the channel list, so you pick where to go next.

### Fixes
- Phones: a camera held upright is shown whole, to you and to others, instead of a cropped band that looked aimed upward.
- Android app: the first time you join voice, the microphone question comes before the notification question, so the microphone works straight away.
- Microphone and camera problems now say what actually went wrong (refused, not found, can't be opened, or can't reach the voice server) instead of always "check your permissions".
- Back goes up one step everywhere: in first run, in menus, and it keeps unsaved settings and a forum post you are writing.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.46.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** create one in the desktop app, rent one from the app, or run the `iskra-server` package: `iskra-server my-server`, then open the owner link it prints. The [hosting guide](https://iskra.foo/hosting) has every step.
