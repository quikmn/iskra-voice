**Iskra: alpha 40.**

### What's new
- Android: calls play on the speaker, like on iPhone. A new speaker button in the call switches to the earpiece (hold the phone to your ear) and back; a headset or Bluetooth takes over by itself.
- Phones: a button switches between the front and back camera while your camera is on.

### Fixes
- GIFs sent through your server work: every GIF from KLIPY was refused with "Couldn't send", because KLIPY's long addresses didn't fit the server's check.
- GIFs you send yourself show right away, also when your server loads GIFs straight from KLIPY. Other people's get an "Always load GIFs on this device" link on the click-to-load box.
- Encrypted channels no longer offer GIFs and stickers, which can't be sent there yet (they used to fail with "Couldn't send").
- Turning on GIFs: the steps in Server settings and the hosting guide now match KLIPY's site, click by click.
- A phone's camera shows your whole face again: a phone held upright sends a tall picture, which was cut down to its middle in the call, so you had to tilt the phone.
- Phones keep the screen on while your camera is on in a call, so the call doesn't lose your camera when the phone would lock (also with a work profile's lock timeout).

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.40.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** create one in the desktop app, rent one from the app, or run the `iskra-server` package: `iskra-server my-server`, then open the owner link it prints. The [hosting guide](https://iskra.foo/hosting) has every step.
