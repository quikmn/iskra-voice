**Iskra: alpha 33.**

### What's new
- Four more built-in bots: Tags posts saved snippets with /tag, Suggestions collects ideas with thumbs up and down votes that staff can accept or decline, Giveaways runs giveaways with a fair draw on your own server, and Counting turns a channel into a counting game (no XP, just the group's best streak).

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.33.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** create one in the desktop app, rent one from the app, or run the `iskra-server` package: `iskra-server my-server`, then open the owner link it prints. The [hosting guide](https://github.com/quikmn/iskra-voice/blob/main/docs/hosting.md) has every step.

### Good to know
- Bug reports now come with a clear timeline in the same format as the server's log, so a host can match a member's report to their own log, and the desktop app keeps its own private log (no names, messages, keys or addresses), compressed after 60 days and deleted after 180.
