**Iskra: alpha 26.**

### What's new
- Join-to-create voice rooms: admins can make a voice channel a lobby, and whoever joins it gets a room of their own under it, which they can rename, limit and lock; empty rooms clean themselves up.
- Listen together: watch parties have a queue (links, YouTube and uploads, for anyone who may control the party), move on by themselves when an item ends, and an audio-only mode with a compact now-playing bar, so a voice channel can be a shared jukebox; on phones the queue is a bottom sheet.
- Streamer mode (My settings, or your status menu): hides invite links, account ids, safety numbers and email addresses in profiles, keeps notifications and sounds quiet, and shows a small pill while it's on; the desktop app can turn it on by itself while a program from your list (OBS, Streamlabs, …) is running.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.26.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** create one in the desktop app, rent one from the app, or run the `iskra-server` package: `iskra-server my-server`, then open the owner link it prints. The [hosting guide](https://github.com/quikmn/iskra-voice/blob/main/docs/hosting.md) has every step.
