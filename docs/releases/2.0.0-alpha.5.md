**Iskra: alpha 5.** A huge one.

### What's new
- **Group DMs and group calls** for up to 10 friends: voice, video and screen share, end-to-end encrypted.
- **Files, photos and voice messages in DMs**, end-to-end encrypted. **Voice messages** in channels too.
- **Status:** Online, Idle, Do not disturb, Invisible, plus a custom status. Do not disturb means no sounds and no pushes.
- **Stage channels** for talks and game nights, and **watch parties**: watch a video or YouTube together, in sync.
- **Forum channels** and **scheduled events** with reminders.
- **Organize:** channel categories, per-channel and per-server notification levels and mute, nicknames, server folders, message forwarding, "New messages" marker and Alt+↑/↓.
- **Moderation:** custom roles with permissions and a Moderator preset, @role mentions, timeouts, slowmode, an audit log, and blocking.
- **Voice:** strong noise suppression, full sound settings (devices, volumes, sensitivity, mic test), per-person volume, and your own speaking ring.
- **Desktop app:** About and Check for updates in the tray, unread badge on the taskbar, start with your computer, and screen share in up to 1080p60 with sound.
- **Servers:** encrypted backups, move a server to another PC, and import history from Discord (DiscordChatExporter).
- **Security:** replace your account key if it ever leaks, keys encrypted on your device, optional app lock.
- Linking a new device brings your message history along.
- **Chat:** headings, lists, highlighted code, timestamps in everyone's own time, image grids with a swipeable gallery, spoilers, stickers, GIFs (KLIPY), click-to-play YouTube and Twitch, message links, "Mark unread", drafts.
- **"Playing X"** activity status (opt-in), and screen sharing with sound that leaves out the call itself.
- Messages sent on a shaky connection arrive exactly once.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.5.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** the `iskra-server` packages. Run `iskra-server my-server` and open the owner link it prints.
