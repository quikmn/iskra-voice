**Iskra: alpha 43.**

### What's new
- A calmer app frame: the server menu is shorter (the server, you here, leave; everything else under More settings, in groups) with your notifications on one page; the member list has a header with a count and a search; switching or adding accounts lives in your status menu, and the rail shows a stacked account tile only when you have more than one; the recovery key reminder is a slim line, then a dot on your avatar; Invite friends is a big card while you are alone; a server that can't be reached or waits for approval shows one centred card.
- Calls look calmer: the controls sit on one row in three groups (you, together, the call), with watch together, whiteboard, activities and the soundboard in one "Together" popover and a little arrow by the mic and camera to pick a device. On phones it's five big buttons and a sheet for the rest. Video tiles fill the call screen, the voice card in the sidebar is tidier, and Draw & guess, the activity list and watch party controls got a cleaner layout.
- Chat looks calmer: channel names stay readable in a crowded header (extra tools fold into one "⋯"), the composer has one "+" for files, polls, voice messages and send later, a message's menu is grouped with an icon on every row on desktop and phones, friend requests show as a banner in a DM, and the profile card keeps "Copy user ID" in its "⋯".

### Fixes
- Draw & guess: the drawing shows for everyone again, not only for the person drawing.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.43.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** create one in the desktop app, rent one from the app, or run the `iskra-server` package: `iskra-server my-server`, then open the owner link it prints. The [hosting guide](https://iskra.foo/hosting) has every step.
