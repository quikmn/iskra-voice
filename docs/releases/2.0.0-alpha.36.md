**Iskra: alpha 36.**

### What's new
- Several accounts in one app: add another account from the bottom of the server list (create a new one or use one you already have) and switch between them. Every account stays connected and notifies you, and the badge counts them all. One account can be in voice at a time; joining with another moves you over, with "Join here" to switch back.

- Record a voice channel in the desktop app: everyone gets their own audio file, saved on your computer and never uploaded. Everyone in the call sees a red Recording mark, joins muted with a heads-up, and can say "Don't record me". Encrypted channels are only recorded when everyone agrees. Admins have the new "Record voice" permission; give it to others in Roles.

- Share to Iskra from any Android app: photos, videos, files, links and text go straight to a channel, a DM or a group, encrypted wherever the conversation is.

### Fixes
- Joining voice with the same account on a second device now says "Moved to your other device", with "Join here" to take it back.
- Being moved to the AFK channel no longer drops you out of voice now and then.
- A voice pop-out stays on top of full-screen games on Windows.
- "Now playing" no longer flickers on a busy computer.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.36.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** create one in the desktop app, rent one from the app, or run the `iskra-server` package: `iskra-server my-server`, then open the owner link it prints. The [hosting guide](https://iskra.foo/hosting) has every step.

### Good to know
- Only Iskra apps that show the Recording mark are ever recorded: people on older apps are left out of recordings until they update.
