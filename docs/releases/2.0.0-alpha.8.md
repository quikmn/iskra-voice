**Iskra: alpha 8.** A big one.

### What's new
- **Android app.** Iskra full screen on your phone, with notifications, voice and video: `Iskra-2.0.0-alpha.8.apk`. On iPhone, Iskra now walks you through adding it to your Home Screen.
- **Svenska!** Iskra speaks Swedish. It follows your system language, or pick one under Settings → Appearance. The desktop app's tray and dialogs follow along.
- **Mute and deafen keybinds** (Settings → Voice & audio). In the desktop app they work while a game has focus, including key combos and mouse side buttons.
- **AutoMod** (Server settings → AutoMod): block words and phrases, mention spam, floods, repeated messages and invite links. A blocked message is never posted; moderators can get an alert, and AutoMod can time people out.
- **Server rules and a welcome screen.** New members see the rules and agree before they chat or join voice. Ask everyone to agree again when the rules change.
- **Voice channel user limits.** Set "max 5" on any voice channel; everyone sees "2/5" in the channel list. Moderators can still join.
- **Unread, done right.** The sidebar, server icons, taskbar count and the "New messages" line always agree, in channels, threads, DMs and group chats.
- **Twitch** clips and streams now play right inside the desktop app.

### Fixes
- Your own message no longer shows twice for a moment while it sends.
- A Discord import can be retried right away after it finishes or fails.

### Get started
- **Windows:** `Iskra-Setup-2.0.0-alpha.8.exe`. **Mac:** the `.dmg`. **Linux:** AppImage, `.deb` or `.rpm`. **Android:** the `.apk`.
- **In the browser:** [app.iskra.foo](https://app.iskra.foo).
- **Your own server:** the `iskra-server` packages. Run `iskra-server my-server` and open the owner link it prints.

### Good to know
- Apps from alpha 3 and later update themselves. Servers don't: download the new `iskra-server` and restart it.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
