    Keys: Space play/pause, 0 original, 1–6 A–F (A, B, X in ABX), ← → 2 s back/ahead, Home to the start. All versions play in sync: switching is instant and keeps your place. It loops.

    Score this clip

    Each letter is one of six settings, shuffled for every clip. Compare each with the original as often as you like. 5 = as good as the original (for artefacts: none at all), 1 = bad.

    Consonants: s, t, k, f, sh crisp and natural. Word ends: endings kept, not swallowed or chopped. Pacing: natural timing, nothing hurried, stretched or skipped. Steady level: no pumping, dips or jumps. Artefacts: 5 = none; watery, metallic or robotic sound, gating, breathing noise floor and the like bring it down.

    ABX: can you hear a difference?

    Pick two settings. X is secretly one of them, picked anew for every trial. Listen to A, B and X (switch as often as you like) and say which one X is. 10 trials; 9 or more right means a real difference (less than 1 in 100 by guessing), 8 is likely one.
